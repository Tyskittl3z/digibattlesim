# Rina actual-suspension rules review

The saved command105 may suspend B:EX13-023#1 after consuming its orientation OPT. This triggers B BT11 Rina and inherited BT22 Aero simultaneously; B chooses their order. Both derived reactions precede original return effect35 (CR15-4-3-4/5;15-4-5-2).

Rina self-suspension is optional. An already suspended Rina cannot pay (CR1-3-3;15-7-4). An unsuspended Rina may pay even with no eligible When Digivolving effect: **Q2142 explicitly says yes, suspend the Tamer and activate none**; CR15-7-5 permits paying when the resulting processing cannot execute. Do not invent a no-target prohibition. Declining the cost activates no When Digivolving effect.

If paid, activate one eligible current-top When Digivolving effect, retaining its own optional processing. Q7199 forbids a used OPT; Q7200 allows selecting one of several. Here orientation has just used its shared OPT, leaving only the non-OPT return line eligible. The separate original return effect35 remains pending. CR15-15-7-1 requires the selected effect to process during Rina resolution; it cannot be deferred behind Aero or effect35. This is direct effect activation: no evolution cost, bonus draw, or retriggering every When Digivolving line.

Track the observing Rina and observed Digimon by entity. Q2143 expressly allows the same Digimon to evolve first and then activate its new top’s effect, even after losing the Veedramon name; its new actual evolution effects resolve before older Rina. CR3-4-5 preserves identity through evolution; leaving and returning creates a new card (CR3-1-3-1). Aero unsuspending first does not undo the historical suspension event. New unsuspend reactions resolve before older effects, and a subsequent actual evolution may change Rina’s eligible selection.

In the saved state, B BT11 Rina’s turn8 unsuspend-memory OPT is already used. EX1 Veemon only gains memory during own main phase, so it does not fire in UNSUSPEND. EX13 Rina#3 may pay on a new actual unsuspend despite declining previously; Rina#4 remains unable to pay while suspended. The egg only observes Tamer plays.
