# EX13 imported-matchup engine

The user assigned this chat the engine/mechanic packs, balanced 60-match fact-finding,
three-lane roadmaps and frozen 10,000-match evaluations. The other chat owns catalog,
generic import, legality/comparison/storage and browser UI. On October 30 the user
selected the uploaded EX13 workbook, requested randomized opponents, confirmed
60/10,000 applies per pairing, and explicitly directed execution.

## Approach

Keep the old loaded-pair engine as a reproducible diagnostic implementation. Add
an explicit immutable matchup context and a scoped mechanic dispatch boundary;
do not monkey-patch global BY_SEAT, substitute old pilots, or infer effects from
text. A complete rewrite or wholesale renaming of the old matchup would obscure
its unsupported paths. The explicit-context approach permits independent imports
without certifying new gameplay. This is the implementer's choice under the
user's direction to get started, not an additional user-selected design.

## Contracts

- Exact composition identities include main and egg zones and quantities.
- Card definitions come from pinned source bytes; third-party transcriptions are
  provisional until compared with official text and Q&A. No favorable judge
  attestations are generated by software.
- A context binds ordered seats, card definitions, rules/format selections and
  mechanic implementations. Changing any of these creates a fresh context.
- Pilots, roadmaps, matches and schedules belong to that context. Prior matchup
  evidence cannot supply new-matchup learning or approvals.
- Mechanic dispatch requires an exact card/effect/text binding and pinned official
  evidence; unsupported calls halt before mutation. Unreviewed software packs
  remain EXPERIMENTAL. Runtime integrity and judge certification stay separate.
- Core state conserves each physical copy, tracks ordered stacks, separates both
  players' hidden information, and rejects actions after terminal state.
- Preserve the random queue already drawn (1,431 distinct unordered pairs from
  54 entries). The queue is a reproducible ordering, not authorization to claim
  evaluation completion or a frozen gameplay seed schedule. First pairing is
  UlforceVeedramon 123129 against Gallantmon 124303. Same-archetype variants may
  meet; an exact entry never meets itself.
- Each pairing needs fresh balanced 60+ full matches, reviewed encountered branch
  coverage, evidence-backed best/medium/worst lanes, and a new gated freeze before
  10,000 actually played matches. No result may come from a scaffold or scenario.

## Delivery boundaries

New modules and matchup artifacts stay separate from APP and catalog/import work.
The first implementation supplies context, physical-state/audit primitives,
mechanic-pack dispatch and an inspectable selected-matchup readiness inventory.
These are dependencies, not completion of the requested gameplay evaluation.
Independent rules review and any missing official decisions remain real blockers;
do not change the old gate to accept incomplete new-matchup evidence.
