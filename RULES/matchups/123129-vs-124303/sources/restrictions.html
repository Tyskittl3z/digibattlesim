Banned and Restricted Card Announcement
(Aug. 21, 2026)

In the Digimon Card Game, your deck can include up to 4 copies of a card with the same card number. In official tournaments, however, some cards are banned or restricted, limiting the number of copies you can play.

Banned cards: Can’t be included in decks.

Restricted cards (1): You can only include a number of copies equal to the number listed in parentheses.

Banned Pair: If A is included in a deck, B is banned from being included in the deck.

Note:
Banned and restricted cards listed here may be removed from the list depending on the current competitive environment.

Based on the competitive environment, the following cards will be added tothe banned and restricted list.

List of Currently Affected Cards

Effective on Sep. 1, 2026

Banned cards: Can’t be included in decks.
  • *Even if the illustrations are different, cards with the same card number cannot be included in the same deck.
BT15-003
Nyaromon

As the game's card pool grows, Nyaromon's [When Attacking] effect continues to contribute to an increasingly fast game pace by not only offering the ability to trash 1 security card to gain 1 memory, but also enabling other advantages gained by trashing your own security.
Yellow cards, as well as cards that have effects related to security, both have their future freedom of design limited as a result.
We considered a 1 card restriction, but as this is a Digi-Egg card, it can be easily used at the desired timing, making a 1 card restriction insufficient.
For the above reasons, this card will be banned.

Restricted Cards (1): Decks can only include one copy of these cards.
  • *Even the illustrations are different, if they have the same card number that can only include one copy of these cards.
EX1-066
Analog Youth

While this card has a general effect that increases the stability of decks, in the case of decks that mainly aim to delete your own Digimon, its [All Turns] effect is very effective at hatching Digi-Eggs, contributing to a faster game pace.
The decks described above, as well as cards that interact with the trash, both have their future freedom of design limited as a result. This led to our determination that this card is overtuned for its generalist role.
For the above reasons, this card is restricted to 1 per deck.

EX8-012
Growlmon (X Antibody)

This single card contains many necessary elements for decks that interact with the trash or decks with mechanics that delete your own Digimon, contributing to a lack of diversity in the cards played.
For the above reasons, this card is restricted to 1 per deck.

BT14-033
Patamon

As the game's card pool grows, this card's [Start of your Main Phase] effect, which allows you to cheat in big Digimon via effortless digivolution, and its ability to place a desired card from your hand in your security stack, continue to offer great advantages.

At the same time its inherited effect, which provides 1 memory whenever a card is added to your security stack, is easy to trigger multiple times, and contributes to a faster game pace.

As a result, [Vaccine] trait cards, and cards with [Security] effects have their future freedom of design limited.
For the above reasons, this card is restricted to 1 per deck.
